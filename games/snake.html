Math Snake — Play Free Online Game

Math Snake is an addictive browser-based math arcade game where players navigate a growing snake across a grid to eat correct arithmetic answer balls. Combine retro snake mechanics with addition, subtraction, and multiplication training.

🎮 How to Play Math Snake

Use Arrow keys or WASD on desktop, or swipe / turn the touch steering wheel on mobile devices to direct the snake towards the green food ball displaying the correct math answer. Avoid crashing into walls or eating wrong red answer balls.

⚡ Tips & High Score Strategies

Always plan your path two turns ahead. If the correct food ball is near a wall, slow down your turn to avoid wall collisions.
