Carrom Board — Play Free Online Carrom Pool Game

Carrom Board Pool is a realistic 2D physics board game. Position your striker, aim, and shoot to pocket White pucks (+10), Black pucks (+5), and cover the Red Queen (+25) across 4 corner pockets!

🎮 How to Play Carrom Board

Drag your striker along the bottom baseline to position it. Pull back to set your shot power and aim trajectory line, then release to strike!

⚡ Tips & Carrom Pool Strategies

Target corner pucks first to open up the center circle, then aim for bank shots off the wooden cushions to pocket the Red Queen!
