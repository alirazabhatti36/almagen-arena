Snakes and Ladders — Play Free Online Board Game

Snakes and Ladders (Saanp Seerhi) is a classic 1-100 tile family board game. Roll dice, climb ladders, dodge snakes, and race your pawn to tile 100 against the computer!

🎮 How to Play Snakes and Ladders

Click Roll Dice or press Spacebar to spin the die. Move your pawn across the 100 tiles. Land on a ladder base to climb up, but watch out for snake heads that slide you down!
