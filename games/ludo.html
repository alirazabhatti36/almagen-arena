Ludo Board — Play Free Online Classic Board Game

Ludo Board is the authentic 15x15 four-corner classic board game. Roll ⚅ 6 to bring tokens out, race 4 tokens along the perimeter track, knockout opponents, and reach the center triangle!

🎮 How to Play Ludo Board

Click Roll Dice or press Spacebar to spin the die. Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your home base. Move your tokens around the board onto safe star cells ⭐ and race to the center home triangle!
