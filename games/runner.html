Life Runner — Stage-Based Educational Cyber Runner

Life Runner is a high-octane 2D endless cyber runner designed to train reflexes, habit-building, and decision making. Jump, slide, and dodge life distractions across 3 unique stages while collecting positive habit powerups and protective shields.

🎮 How to Play Life Runner

Use Arrow Up / Down or W/S to switch lanes. Collect green powerups (Salad, Books, Master Gems) and 🛡️ Shields to increase your score and advance through Stage 1, Stage 2, and Stage 3!

⚡ Tips & Stage Completion Strategies

Prioritize collecting 🛡️ Shield powerups to absorb distraction hits without losing your score progress!
