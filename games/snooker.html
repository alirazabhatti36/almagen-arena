Snooker Champion 2D Pool — Play Free Online Cue Sports

Snooker Champion is an authentic 2D pool snooker simulator. Aim your cue stick, adjust shot power, strike red (+1 pt) & colored balls (+2 to +7 pts) into 6 pockets with realistic physics!

🎮 How to Play Snooker Champion

Drag your mouse or finger across the green baize table to rotate cue aiming line. Use the Power Slider to select force (10% - 100%), then click SHOOT to strike the white cue ball!
