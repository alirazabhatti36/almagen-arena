Street Racer — High-Speed Educational Math Highway Racing

Street Racer is a fast-paced educational 2D highway math racing game. Steer your sports car through correct answer gates, unlock nitro turbo boosts, dodge rival traffic, and complete 3 highway stages!

🎮 How to Play Street Racer

Use Left / Right Arrow keys or A/D (or tap mobile buttons / drag canvas) to steer your car. Solve the Checkpoint Math equation at the top and drive into the green lane displaying the correct answer to trigger Nitro Boosts!

⚡ Tips & Stage Completion Strategies

Focus on solving the Checkpoint Math equation early so you have time to steer around rival cars into the green answer gate!
